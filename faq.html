Frequenty Asked Questions

01

What is AI in healthcare?

AI in healthcare refers to using machine learning, robotics, and automation to improve diagnosis, treatment, and hospital operations.

02

How does automation improve hospitals?

Automation reduces administrative burden by handling billing, scheduling, patient records, and even medication dispensing.

03

Is AI replacing doctors?

No. AI supports doctors by providing faster and more accurate insights, but human expertise is still essential.

04

What are the risks of AI in healthcare?

Challenges include data privacy concerns, high implementation costs, and potential bias in AI algorithms.

05

Can patients trust AI diagnoses?

AI is highly accurate in certain areas (like imaging and pathology), but final decisions should always be made by healthcare professionals.

06

How is telemedicine connected to AI?

AI enhances telemedicine by providing chatbots, remote monitoring tools, and diagnostic support during online consultations.

07

What’s the future of AI in healthcare?

Expect more personalized medicine, wearable tech integration, and advanced robotic surgeries.
